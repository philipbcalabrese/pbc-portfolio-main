About Me

Hi! I am Philip Brennan-Calabrese. I am a Chicago-based actor in my final year of the BFA Acting program at The Theatre School at DePaul University.

Growing up in the suburbs of Chicago in an Irish-Italian household, art has always been the biggest consistency in my life. Since I was a young boy, I loved to listen to music and dance along to music videos with my family. After seeing a movie in theaters, I was eager to run home and reenact what I just watched. All of this eventually led me to acting and theatre.

My first introduction to theatre was joining the crew for my school musical in fifth grade, where I fell in love with the art form and realized I wanted to try performing myself. With the encouragement of my teacher at the time, I was able to continue my artistic journey to high school, where I truly discovered the importance of art and how it can positively impact someone’s life. Senior year, I realized I couldn’t see myself doing anything else with my life.

I was then accepted into The Theatre School at DePaul University as an Acting major where I’ve spent the last four years honing my craft. As I wrap up my final year and reflect on this wonderful journey I’ve been so fortunate to be on, I realize truly immersing myself in and pursuing my passion to this degree with so many incredible people has made my love for acting stronger than ever.

Some fun facts!

Traveling is one of my favorite things to do! I’ve been so lucky to be able to attend a The 2025 Greece study abroad program
